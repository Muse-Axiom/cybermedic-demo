Questions practice managers ask us

Straight answers, no jargon.

How do I train my dental staff on HIPAA without boring them to death?

Short, scenario-based sessions beat annual slide decks. Our free Lunch & Learn covers the HIPAA basics every team member needs — what counts as PHI, what not to text, how to handle a curious caller — in 30 minutes over lunch. Staff remember stories, not policies.

What's the biggest cyber risk for a small medical practice?

Phishing aimed at front-desk and billing staff. Attackers know small practices wire money, process insurance, and hold valuable patient records — and that one rushed click can install ransomware. Simulated phishing tests plus short training cut click rates dramatically.

Do we really need a HIPAA risk assessment?

Yes — it's explicitly required by the HIPAA Security Rule, and it's the first thing auditors ask for after an incident. Most small practices have never done one. Ours takes about two weeks and ends with a prioritized fix list in plain English.

How often should staff get phishing training?

At least quarterly, in short sessions — annual training doesn't stick. Monthly simulated phishing emails with instant 2-minute coaching when someone clicks is the pattern that actually changes behavior.

What should we do in the first 10 minutes of a suspected breach?

Don't turn everything off (you'll destroy evidence). Isolate the affected machine from the network, call your IT/security contact, and start a written timeline of what happened. We give every client a one-page version of this plan.
